# Vernacular (Hindi/Hinglish) Social Copywriting for Indian SMB Audiences, 2025–2026: Captions, Hooks, CTAs, Hashtags

Context: FutureTech Digital Marketing Agency (Sagar, MP). It sells websites, custom web apps/ERP/billing software, local SEO/GBP, and Google/Meta ads to local MP businesses. In its own data, a Hindi problem-solution video ("Kya aapka vyapar bhi aaj tak purane bahi-khaate mein uljha hai…") reached 5–10x more people than its English posts. That is a first-party, single-account observation, not a controlled test.

Research date: 2 Nov 2026. Older data is labelled.

---

## 1. Evidence on vernacular content performance in India (Hindi vs English; Devanagari vs Roman Hinglish)

### Takeaway
Macro data strongly supports vernacular-first content. The IAMAI-Kantar Internet in India 2024 report (Jan 2025) found ~98% of Indian internet users consume content in Indic languages, and rural users (55% of the base) drive growth. The 2025 edition (Feb 2026) counts ~958M users, 61% of whom watch short video. No rigorous public study directly compares engagement for Hindi vs English posts, or for Devanagari vs Roman script. The often-quoted lift multiples ("3–5x", "1.5–2x") trace back to vendor blogs, not primary Meta or Google publications. The agency's own 5–10x result is consistent with the macro picture and is its best evidence.

### Inferences
- For a Sagar/Bundelkhand SMB audience (shopkeepers, krishi kendra owners, clinic doctors, coaching owners), Hindi voice-over and on-screen text is the default. English should be the exception, used for tech terms people already say in English (website, software, billing, Google, ads, GST, QR).
- **Script choice:** use **Roman Hinglish for captions** (faster to type, matches how owners write on WhatsApp, and searchable by both Hindi and English keywords). Use **Devanagari for big on-screen text and thumbnails** (high visual impact, signals "this is for Hindi-speaking business owners", and reads well for older owners). Then A/B test. No evidence supports one script beating the other on engagement, so the agency's own tests will be the best guide.
- The agency's 5–10x reach result is most likely a mix of language, format (Reel/video vs static), and relatable problem framing. Future tests should change one variable at a time.
- Hindi-only captions can lose English keyword discoverability in Instagram and Google search. Including both Hindi words (dukaan, billing software) and English service keywords (website design Sagar) covers both.

### Gaps

- No rigorous public study compares Devanagari vs Roman Hinglish engagement on Instagram or Facebook.
- The 2025 IAMAI-Kantar report's regional-language preference figure was not found in the coverage.
- No 2024–2026 ShareChat/Moj/Josh user reports with verified figures were found.

## 2. Hook formulas for Reels and captions (with 20 Hinglish hooks for FutureTech)

### Takeaway
None of the sources found tests hook formulas specifically for Indian SMB owners. The working formulas below are standard copywriting patterns, adapted to the agency's proven winner: a relatable pain-point question in Hindi with a local or traditional reference ("bahi-khaata"). Instagram's own guidance supports clear on-topic text, because text drives search. Meta's Reels emphasis supports hooking in the first 1–3 seconds, using spoken audio plus on-screen text.

### Cited Findings
- On Reels, caption length barely affects engagement (PostPlanify, 4,408 posts). That means the spoken and on-screen hook, not the caption, carries a Reel. — [PostPlanify](https://postplanify.com/blog/instagram-caption-length)
- Meta says Reels ads deliver 2x stronger top-of-mind recall and 4x stronger message association than long-form video ads (Meta self-reported). — [Stacked Marketer](https://www.stackedmarketer.com/news/reels-grab-more-and-more-ad-space-and-ai-powered-translations-expand-to-more-languages/)
- The agency's own data shows a Hindi problem-solution hook ("Kya aapka vyapar bhi…") produced 5–10x reach versus English posts (first-party, from the brief).

### Inferences: hook formulas (pattern → why it fits MP SMBs)
1. **Pain-point question** ("Kya aap bhi…?") gets the viewer to identify with the problem. This is the format of the proven winner.
2. **Myth-busting** ("Sabse bada jhooth: …") challenges beliefs like "website mehenga hai" or "Google pe aana sirf badi company ke liye hai".
3. **Number/money hook** ("₹X ka nuksaan…", "3 galtiyan…") is concrete and suits price-conscious owners. Never invent stats. Use real client numbers with permission, or plain arithmetic.
4. **Local reference** (Sagar, Katra Bazaar, Makronia, Tilakganj, Bina, Khurai, Damoh, Chhatarpur, "Bundelkhand ke dukaandaar") gives instant relevance. Verify place names and spellings locally before use.
5. **Before/after or "old vs new"** (bahi-khaata vs billing app; parchi vs WhatsApp order) is a visual contrast.
6. **Customer-voice / overheard** ("Ek customer ne pucha…") feels authentic.
7. **Direct-address by trade** ("Krishi kendra wale bhaiyon dhyaan dein") is a scroll-stopper because of self-selection.
8. **Fear of missing out / competitor** ("Aapke bagal wali dukaan Google pe dikh rahi hai, aap kyun nahi?").

**20 example hooks (Hinglish, Roman script; Devanagari variants for on-screen text):**
1. "Kya aapka vyapar bhi aaj tak purane bahi-khaate mein uljha hai?" (proven winner, reuse as a series)
2. "Sagar mein 'near me' search karne wale customer aapki dukaan tak kyun nahi pahunch rahe?"
3. "Google pe aapki dukaan ka naam search kiya… aur kuch nahi aaya? Ye 1 galti theek kijiye."
4. "Sabse bada jhooth: 'Website banwana bahut mehenga hai.'"
5. "Roz ka hisaab raat 11 baje tak? Billing software se 10 minute mein khatam."
6. "Krishi kendra wale bhaiyon — udhaar ka hisaab diary mein ya mobile mein?"
7. "Clinic ke bahar line hai, par Google pe 3 star? Patient yahi dekh ke aate hain."
8. "Coaching admission season aa raha hai — parents aapko Google pe dhoondh paayenge?"
9. "₹500 roz Facebook ads pe kharch ho rahe hain, par ek bhi call nahi? Ye 3 cheezein check kijiye."
10. "Aapke bagal wali dukaan ko WhatsApp pe order aa rahe hain. Aapko kyun nahi?"
11. "GST bill haath se banate ho? Ek galti aur notice pakka."
12. "Stock khatam hone ka pata customer ke aane ke baad chalta hai? Ye system dekhiye."
13. "Google Business Profile free hai — phir bhi 80% dukaanein ise adhoora chhod deti hain?" (Replace "80%" with a verified local audit count, e.g., "humne Sagar ki 50 dukaanein check ki, 30 ka profile adhoora tha". Do not publish an unverified percentage.)
14. "Sirf Instagram page = online business? Nahi. Ye samjhiye."
15. "Customer ne pucha 'aapki website hai?' — aur aap chup ho gaye?"
16. "Ek chhoti si dukaan, 0 se 100 Google reviews tak — kaise?" (case-study hook, real client only)
17. "Diwali pe offer lagaya, par kisi ko pata hi nahi chala? Ads aise chalaiye."
18. "Purana software, roz crash? Aapke kaam ke hisaab se apna software banwaiye."
19. "Website hai par Google pe 5ve page pe? Matlab website hai hi nahi."
20. "Bundelkhand ke dukaandaar, 2026 mein bhi sirf mohalle ke bharose?"

**Devanagari on-screen variants:**
- "क्या आपका व्यापार भी आज तक पुराने बही-खाते में उलझा है?"
- "सागर में 'near me' सर्च करने वाले ग्राहक आप तक क्यों नहीं पहुँच रहे?"
- "वेबसाइट महंगी है? सबसे बड़ा झूठ।"
- "उधार का हिसाब डायरी में या मोबाइल में?"
- "Google पर आपकी दुकान दिखती है?"

### Gaps
- No India-specific, published A/B data on which hook archetype performs best for SMB B2B audiences.
- Retention and 3-second-hold benchmarks for Hindi Reels were not found in primary sources.

## 3. Caption structure, ideal length, first line, emoji, line breaks

### Takeaway
About 125 characters of an Instagram caption show before "…more", so the first line must carry the hook plus a keyword. Data suggests short captions win on feed posts. Caption length barely matters on Reels, and some research finds an inverted-U, where medium length works best. A skimmable structure works well: hook → 2–4 short lines → keyword-rich detail → single CTA → up to 5 hashtags.

### Cited Findings
- Instagram's caption limit is 2,200 characters, and hashtags count toward it. Roughly 125 characters are visible before "More". One guide cites 125–150 characters (under 30 words) as the best-performing range. — [Ocoya](https://ocoya.com/blog/instagram-caption-length)
- Socialinsider's analysis found engagement potential tends to fall as Instagram caption length increases. — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/blog/instagram-caption-length/)
- PostPlanify (4,408 posts): caption length barely matters for Reels but matters a lot for feed posts. — [PostPlanify](https://postplanify.com/blog/instagram-caption-length)
- Academic study of Facebook and Instagram: the relationship between text length and engagement follows an inverted U-shape, not a linear one. — [opus4.kobv.de (Brusch)](https://opus4.kobv.de/opus4-UBICO/solrsearch/index/search/searchtype/authorsearch/author/Michael+Brusch)
- Instagram: "For a post to be found in Search, put keywords and hashtags in the caption, not the comments." — [Instagram blog (Mosseri, Aug 2021)](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/break-down-how-instagram-search-works/)

### Inferences
**Recommended caption template (Hinglish):**
```
[Line 1 – hook + keyword, ≤125 chars] Kya aapki dukaan Google pe dikhti hai? 📍
[blank line]
[2–4 lines – problem → solution, one idea per line]
Sagar mein roz hazaaron log "near me" search karte hain.
Agar aapka Google Business Profile adhoora hai, customer bagal wali dukaan chala jaata hai.
Hum profile set karte hain: photos, timing, reviews, WhatsApp button.
[blank line]
[proof/offer line] ✅ Free GBP check — 10 minute mein report.
[CTA – one only] 👉 WhatsApp karein: "GBP" likh ke bhejein (link bio mein)
[blank line]
[keywords line, natural] Google Business Profile Sagar | local SEO Madhya Pradesh
#SagarBusiness #LocalSEO #GoogleBusinessProfile #SagarMP #DigitalIndia
```
- **Length:** Reels 1–3 short lines plus CTA. Carousels and educational feed posts can go longer (~300–800 characters) when each line adds value, which is consistent with the inverted-U finding. Static offer posts should be short.
- **Emojis:** use 1 per line at most, as functional markers (📍 location, ✅ benefit, 👉 CTA, 📞/💬 contact). Avoid emoji walls, which read as spam to older business owners. No source quantifies emoji impact for the Indian SMB audience, so treat this as a style guideline.
- **Line breaks:** use short lines and blank lines between blocks. Most viewers are on mobile.
- **Language mix:** Hindi sentence structure, with English for service nouns people search (website, billing software, Google ads). Keep one Devanagari line in some posts as an A/B test.
- **Facebook:** the same caption works. The first ~1–2 lines show before "See more", so the same hook rule applies.

### Gaps
- No India- or Hindi-specific caption-length study was found. Benchmarks are global vendor datasets.
- No 2025–2026 data on emoji use and engagement in Indian business posts was found.

## 4. CTA best practices (WhatsApp click-to-chat, "DM karein", comment keywords) and Meta's engagement-bait stance

### Takeaway
WhatsApp is the most natural CTA for Indian SMB owners. Meta reports that >60% of people on WhatsApp in India message a business account weekly, and India's click-to-message ad revenue doubled YoY. Meta's Facebook Content Distribution Guidelines demote "engagement bait", meaning posts that ask for likes, comments, shares, tags or votes *without a specific call to action*. Genuine CTAs ("WhatsApp karein for a free audit") are fine. "Comment YES if you agree" style prompts risk demotion.

### Cited Findings
- **Meta Engagement Bait guideline (Transparency Center):** covers posts that "explicitly ask people to engage, such as by voting, sharing, commenting, tagging, liking, or using other reactions," when this isn't a specific call to action. It does not apply to posts asking people to show support for an issue, time-sensitive disaster or safety information, or calls to action like finding missing people, raising money or sharing a petition. — [Meta Transparency Center](https://transparency.meta.com/features/approach-to-ranking/content-distribution-guidelines/engagement-bait)
- Facebook has demoted engagement-bait posts since Dec 2017, using ML models trained on hundreds of thousands of posts, with stronger page-level demotion for repeat offenders. Since 2019 it also demotes videos with engagement bait in the audio. — [Meta Newsroom 2017](https://about.fb.com/news/2017/12/news-feed-fyi-fighting-engagement-bait-on-facebook/); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/2017-12-18-facebook-demoting-engagement-bait.html)
- Zuckerberg said revenue from click-to-message ads in India has doubled YoY, and that >60% of people on WhatsApp in India message a business account every week. — [Sentinel Assam](https://sentinelassam.com/business/revenue-from-click-to-message-ads-in-india-has-doubled-meta-founder-672693); [Benzinga India](https://in.benzinga.com/content/35431742/mark-zuckerberg-cheers-success-of-this-whatsapp-feature-in-india)
- Meta committed to train Indian small businesses, including in-person sessions in Tier 2/3 cities, on WhatsApp Business setup, catalogs and ads that click to WhatsApp. — [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/watch-listen/meta-eyes-india-for-business-growth-with-whatsapp-updates-says-vp-sandhya-devanathan-43105.htm)
- WhatsApp began showing ads (in Updates/Status) in June 2025. In Sept 2025, click-to-message ads could also route to WhatsApp Status. — [Storyboard18](https://stgwww.storyboard18.com/amp/digital/meta-expands-ad-opportunities-on-whatsapp-status-81005.htm); [Inc42](https://inc42.com/buzz/now-whatsapp-to-show-ads/)
- Meta Verified and customised messages for small businesses on WhatsApp Business launched in India (Sept 2024). — [TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2024/09/12/whatsapp-brings-meta-verified-customized-messages-to-small-businesses-in-india)
- Unverified vendor claims, not to be cited: "45–60% conversion for CTWA in India" and "200,000 SMBs/month using CTWA". — [exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/ipl-2025-news/breaking-boundaries-how-click-to-message-marketing-is-flipping-the-ipl-playbook-142005.html); [Wisoft](https://www.wisoftsolutions.com/blog/whatsapp-conversion-tips-for-business)

### Inferences
**CTA hierarchy for FutureTech:**
1. **WhatsApp click-to-chat** (bio link `wa.me/91XXXXXXXXXX?text=...` with a pre-filled message, plus Click-to-WhatsApp ads). Example: "👉 WhatsApp karein — 'WEBSITE' likh ke bhejein, free demo bhejenge." The pre-filled text should only hold a keyword such as "WEBSITE" or "BILLING", never personal data.
2. **"DM karein" with a specific benefit**: "Apni dukaan ka naam DM karein — hum free Google profile check karke batayenge."
3. **Comment keyword tied to a real deliverable**: "Comment karein 'BILLING' — hum aapko demo video DM kar denge." This is a specific CTA that delivers something, which is arguably distinct from bait. It still sits close to the line on Facebook, so use it sparingly and actually send the deliverable. Instagram comment-to-DM automation tools exist, but no official Meta statement on them was found.
4. **Call / visit**: "Call karein: 9XXXXXXXXX" or "Office aaiye: [address], Sagar". Strong for older owners.
5. **Save/share for utility**: "Is post ko save kar lijiye — GST bill banate waqt kaam aayega." It is utility-framed. Avoid bare "share karo".

**Avoid (engagement bait):** "Like karo agar aap sehmat ho", "Apne dost ko tag karo", "YES likho agar aap bhi dukaandaar ho", "Share karo warna…", "1 like = 1 dua". Also avoid saying these in Reel audio.

- One CTA per post. Put it in both the caption and the last 2 seconds of the video.

### Gaps
- No Instagram-specific official engagement-bait policy page was found. Meta's published guideline is Facebook-focused, though Meta generally applies similar recommendation guidelines on Instagram (not verified here).
- No India-specific CTWA conversion benchmarks from a primary source were found.
- 2025 WhatsApp per-message pricing changes were not verified.

## 5. Hashtag strategy 2025–2026 (Instagram limit, Mosseri, local tags, Facebook)

### Takeaway
Since mid-December 2025, Instagram caps posts and Reels at **5 hashtags**, after a year of tests with some accounts limited to 3. Instagram itself says to use "fewer (up to 5) more targeted hashtags, rather than many generic ones". Mosseri has repeatedly said hashtags don't meaningfully increase reach and mainly help with categorisation and search. On Facebook, evidence (mostly old) suggests hashtags don't help reach. Keyword-rich captions now matter more than hashtags.

### Cited Findings
- **Social Samosa (19 Dec 2025):** Instagram is capping hashtags in captions on posts and Reels at five, rolled out gradually, after a year of testing during which some users were limited to as few as three. The aim is to curb spam that relies on generic tags like #reels and #explore. Instagram's Threads announcement recommends "using fewer (up to 5) more targeted hashtags, rather than many generic ones." — [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-hashtags-five-per-post-10923075)
- The coverage gives 18 Dec 2025 as the date, says Mosseri confirmed the change, and quotes him that hashtags are not a way to get more reach and mainly help with context and search. — [MyMobileIndia](https://www.mymobileindia.com/instagram-limits-hashtags-to-five-per-post-and-reel-heres-key-changes-creators-should-know/); [IT Voice](https://www.itvoice.in/end-of-an-era-instagram-slashes-hashtag-limits-to-combat-spam-and-engagement-hacking)
- **Conflict:** some sources in Jan 2026 still said the Instagram Help Center referenced 30. Others quote it as "You can use up to 5 tags on a post." Verify at help.instagram.com before finalising. — [Storrito](https://storrito.com/resources/instagram-hashtag-limit-30-to-3-what-users-are-seeing/); [Circleboom](https://circleboom.com/blog/how-many-hashtags-should-you-use-on-instagram-in-2026/); [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/why-instagram-only-lets-you-use-5-hashtags-some-posts/)
- Unverified blog claims: that the cap counts caption plus first comment together, and that over-limit posts are demoted from Explore. Not confirmed by Instagram. — [Lilach Bullock](https://www.lilachbullock.com/instagram-hashtag-limit/)
- **Facebook:** the only sizeable study (EdgeRank Checker, 500+ pages and 35,000+ posts, c. 2013, OLDER DATA) found viral reach of 1.30% without hashtags vs 0.80% with them. 2025–2026 blogs say hashtags have minimal effect on Facebook reach and are mainly useful for campaigns, Groups and cross-posting consistency. — [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/facebook-reach-hashtags/68612); [Radaar](https://radaar.io/resources-121/blog-388/are-facebook-hashtags-still-relevant-in-2025-and-should-you-use-them-11165)

### Inferences
**Suggested 5-hashtag structure (Instagram), "1-1-1-1-1":**
1. **Local/geo (1):** #Sagar / #SagarMP / #SagarMadhyaPradesh (rotate). Optionally #Bundelkhand or #MadhyaPradesh.
2. **Service (1):** #WebsiteDesign / #BillingSoftware / #LocalSEO / #GoogleBusinessProfile / #GoogleAds / #MetaAds
3. **Audience/industry (1):** #DukaanDigital / #KiranaStore / #KrishiKendra / #ClinicMarketing / #CoachingInstitute / #SmallBusinessIndia
4. **Topic/problem (1):** #GSTBilling / #DigitalMarketingTips / #OnlineBusiness / #VyaparBadhao
5. **Branded (1):** #FutureTechSagar (consistent; it builds a searchable archive)

Example: `#SagarMP #BillingSoftware #KrishiKendra #GSTBilling #FutureTechSagar`

- Drop generic tags like #reels, #explore, #viral and #instagood. Instagram explicitly calls these out.
- Check volume and relevance of local tags by searching them in the Instagram app. No public data on Sagar-specific tag volumes was found.
- Hindi hashtags (#व्यापार, #सागर) are optional. Use at most one, as a test.
- **Facebook:** use 0–2 hashtags (branded plus local) at most, mainly for consistency. Put the effort into the first line and the CTA instead.

### Gaps
- No primary Instagram Help Center text was retrieved directly, and sources conflict on whether it said 5 or 30 as of Jan 2026.
- No data on volume or effectiveness of Sagar/MP local hashtags.
- No 2024–2026 rigorous Facebook hashtag-reach study.

## 6. Instagram SEO / keyword search (captions, name field, bio, alt text, Google indexing from July 2025)

### Takeaway
Instagram officially says search text is "by far the most important signal for Search". It matches against usernames, profile names, bios, captions, hashtags and places, and keywords belong in captions, not comments. Since 10 July 2025, public posts from professional (business/creator) accounts of users 18+ can appear in Google (and reportedly Bing) results. So caption keywords, alt text and the profile name now affect discoverability both on and off Instagram.

### Cited Findings
- **Instagram official (Mosseri, Aug 2021, still the canonical explainer):** "The text you enter in the search bar is by far the most important signal for Search." Results are matched on usernames, bios, captions, hashtags and places. Other signals are your activity and popularity (clicks, likes, shares, follows). Tips: "Using an Instagram handle or profile name that's related to the content of your posts is your best bet". Include your location in the bio if you're a local business. "For a post to be found in Search, put keywords and hashtags in the caption, not the comments." — [Instagram blog](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/break-down-how-instagram-search-works/)
- **Google indexing:** from 10 July 2025, public photos, videos, Reels and carousels from professional accounts of users 18+ became eligible for Google indexing. Stories and private accounts are excluded, and accounts can opt out in settings. — [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/amp/digital/instagram-to-make-public-posts-searchable-on-googlebing-74611.htm); [Inman](https://www.inman.com/2025/07/11/with-instagrams-latest-update-posts-can-now-boost-your-seo/); [WordStream](https://www.wordstream.com/blog/instagram-posts-indexed-on-google)
- **Conflict:** some sources say eligible posts were indexed in many regions before July 2025. The July 2025 change is best described as a broadened, default-on rollout for professional accounts. — [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-indexing-on-google/)
- **Name field:** guides call the bold Name field (separate from the @handle) a key searchable field and recommend "Brand | Service" (e.g., "FutureTech | Website & Billing Software Sagar"). One guide says bio keywords are weighted less than the Name field. — [Inro](https://www.inro.social/blog/instagram-seo); [SocialPilot](https://www.socialpilot.co/blog/instagram-seo)
- **Alt text:** guides describe it as both an accessibility feature and a search/indexing signal that now also feeds Google. They advise putting accessibility ahead of keyword stuffing. — [Kontentino](https://www.kontentino.com/q-and-a/instagram-search-seo/); [Metricool](https://metricool.com/instagram-seo/)
- One third-party claim (not verified by Instagram) says Instagram also uses content understanding (embeddings), so posts can rank without exact caption keywords. — [Kontentino](https://www.kontentino.com/q-and-a/instagram-search-seo/)

### Inferences
**Profile setup for FutureTech:**
- **Name field (searchable, 30 chars):** "FutureTech | Website Sagar" or "FutureTech Digital Sagar MP". Test whether "Website & Billing Software" fits.
- **Bio:** "Website • Billing/ERP Software • Google Business Profile • Ads | Sagar, MP 📍 | WhatsApp 👇", plus a wa.me link and the address. Add one Hindi line: "Aapke vyapar ko online laayein".
- **Captions:** put 1–2 natural keyword phrases in the first two lines, e.g., "billing software for kirana store Sagar" or "website design Sagar Madhya Pradesh". Mix Hindi and English: "dukaan ka billing software".
- **Alt text** (Advanced settings → Write alt text): describe the image plus context, e.g., "Sagar ke krishi kendra owner billing software ka demo dekh rahe hain — FutureTech". Avoid stuffing.
- **Location tag:** always tag Sagar or the client's locality.
- **Google angle:** each Reel's caption first line effectively acts like a title for Google. Write it as a searchable question or statement ("Sagar mein website kaise banwaye?"). This also supports the agency's own local SEO.
- Keep the account professional and public, with indexing left ON.

### Gaps
- No 2025 primary Instagram or Mosseri statement specifically on alt text or Name-field weighting was found. Those points come from third-party guides.
- No Meta primary announcement of the July 2025 indexing change was retrieved; it is reported in secondary coverage.
- How Instagram search handles Romanised Hindi vs Devanagari queries is unknown.

## 7. Content pillars and 20 post ideas

### Takeaway
No source prescribes pillars for this niche. The pillars below come from the services, the audiences, and the evidence above: Hindi problem-solution Reels, WhatsApp CTAs, local keywords, and short-video consumption in rural/Tier-2 India (IAMAI-Kantar 2025).

### Cited Findings
- 61% of Indian internet users (~588M) consumed short video in 2025, with rural users slightly ahead of urban. This supports a Reels-first mix. — [Manifest Media](https://manifest-media.in/digital/040226/61-internet-users-in-india-consumed-short-video-content-in-2025-repo.html)
- Reels drives product discovery in India (Meta-commissioned study). — [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/metas-reels-now-drives-81-product-discovery-across-india-study-finds/)
- >60% of Indians on WhatsApp message a business weekly, which supports WhatsApp-centric demos and CTAs. — [Benzinga India](https://in.benzinga.com/content/35431742/mark-zuckerberg-cheers-success-of-this-whatsapp-feature-in-india)
- Local businesses should add their location in the bio, and keywords drive search. — [Instagram blog](https://about.instagram.com/blog/announcements/break-down-how-instagram-search-works/)

### Inferences
**Content pillars (suggested weekly mix for ~5 posts):**
1. **Samasya-Samadhan (Problem → Solution)**, ~30%: the proven Hindi format, e.g., bahi-khaata → billing app.
2. **Seekho (Education/Tips)**, ~25%: GBP tips, GST billing basics, "website vs Instagram page", ads myths.
3. **Saboot (Proof/Case studies/Testimonials)**, ~20%: client videos in Hindi, before/after GBP calls and reviews. Real data only, with permission.
4. **Demo/Behind-the-scenes**, ~15%: screen recordings of the billing/ERP software, the team in Sagar, the website build process.
5. **Local & Festive/Trending**, ~10%: Sagar events, festivals (Diwali, Navratri, Holi, Akshaya Tritiya, rabi/kharif seasons for krishi kendras, admission season for coaching), local pride.

**20 post ideas (format → hook → CTA):**
