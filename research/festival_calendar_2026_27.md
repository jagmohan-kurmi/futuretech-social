# Festival, Holiday and Business-Observance Calendar (Nov 2026 – Dec 2027) for Small Businesses in Sagar / Bundelkhand, Madhya Pradesh

Notes prepared 2 Nov 2026. Weekdays were computed with the system calendar (`date`) and cross-checked against the weekdays printed in the DoPT list and Drik Panchang. Drik Panchang dates are for New Delhi; for Sagar they can shift by a day when a tithi changes near sunrise. Legend: **[L]** = depends on the lunar panchang, so check the local panchang; **[M]** = depends on moon sighting (Islamic), so the date can move by ±1 day; **[F]** = fixed Gregorian date.

## 1. Exact dates of major multi-faith festivals (Nov 2026 – Dec 2027)

### Takeaway
The DoPT 2027 central holiday list (issued 16 July 2026) and Drik Panchang give one consistent set of dates for almost every major festival. **Diwali 2026 falls on Sun 8 Nov 2026** and **Diwali 2027 on Fri 29 Oct 2027.** Four dates differ between sources and need local confirmation before posts are scheduled: Holi 2027 (22 or 23 Mar), Makar Sankranti 2027 (14 or 15 Jan), Mahavir Jayanti 2027 (18 or 19 Apr) and Guru Nanak Jayanti 2027 (13 or 14 Nov). All Islamic dates are subject to moon sighting.

### Inferences
- For Sagar, prefer Drik Panchang/local panchang dates for Hindu festival posts. The DoPT list is drawn up for Delhi offices and sometimes shifts a festival to the following day, e.g. Holi 23 Mar vs Drik's 22 Mar. The safe approach is to post Holika Dahan greetings on the evening of 21 Mar and Holi greetings on the morning of 22 Mar, then confirm with the local panchang or MP GAD list.
- Diwali 2027 falls about 10 days earlier than Diwali 2026. That moves the whole 2027 festive retail season earlier: Navratri starts 30 Sep and Dhanteras is 27 Oct.
- Prepare Islamic festival creatives in advance, but publish only after the moon-sighting announcement the evening before.

### Gaps
- Lohri 2027 was not listed on the Drik page. It is conventionally 13 Jan, the eve of Sankranti. Verify it.
- Jain-specific dates beyond Mahavir Jayanti were not researched in the 15-call budget: Paryushan/Das Lakshan 2027 (likely late Aug–Sep), Kshamavani, and Diwali Nirvan Divas. They matter in Sagar/Damoh because of the large Jain community.
- Buddhist dates beyond Buddha Purnima were not covered.

## 4. Shopping and seasonal peaks: wedding season, agri seasons, exam/admission season

### Takeaway
Drik Panchang (New Delhi) lists 40 vivah muhurats from 21 Nov 2026 to 14 Mar 2027. Weddings pause for Kharmas from mid-Dec 2026 to 14 Jan 2027 and again from 15 Mar 2027. They resume after mid-April, pause again for Chaturmas until Dev Uthani Ekadashi on 10 Nov 2027, and pause for Shukra asta from 20 Jul to 9 Sep 2027. Agri and exam season dates in this section are general patterns and were not source-verified this session.

### Cited Findings
- **Vivah muhurat dates (Drik Panchang, New Delhi):** Nov 2026: 21, 24, 25, 26. Dec 2026: 2, 3, 4, 5, 6, 11, 12. Jan 2027: 15, 18, 19, 20, 24, 26, 27, 30, 31. Feb 2027: 2, 3, 9, 10, 11, 14, 15, 21, 22, 25, 26, 27, 28. Mar 2027: 1, 2, 3, 4, 9, 10, 14. — [HinduTone citing Drik Panchang](https://hindutone.com/astrology/vivah-muhurat-2026-27-shubh-marriage-dates/)
- **Pauses:** Chaturmas ended with Dev Uthani Ekadashi on 20 Nov 2026. Kharmas has no muhurats from about 13 Dec 2026 to 14 Jan 2027; Drik marks Sun in Dhanu from 16 Dec. A second Kharmas (Sun in Meena) starts 15 Mar 2027. Shukra asta runs 20 Jul – 9 Sep 2027. — [HinduTone/Drik](https://hindutone.com/astrology/vivah-muhurat-2026-27-shubh-marriage-dates/)
- Several sources report no muhurats in Aug–Oct 2027 (Chaturmas). Sources disagree on counts: Vaya.so lists 96 dates for 2027, Tempora 57. — [Ganeshaspeaks](https://www.ganeshaspeaks.com/muhurat/2027/marriage-muhurat/); [Vaya.so](https://vaya.so/tools/muhurat/vivah?m=1&y=2027); [Tempora](https://www.tempora.ltd/findings/marriage-muhurat-2027)
- Dev Uthani Ekadashi 2027 is on 10 Nov (Wed). This is the conventional restart of the Nov–Dec 2027 wedding season. — [Drik 2027](https://www.drikpanchang.com/calendars/hindu/hinducalendar.html?year=2027)

### Inferences
- **Wedding-season windows for retail (jewellers, textiles, tent/caterers, salons, photographers):**
  - Peak 1: 21 Nov – 12 Dec 2026.
  - Peak 2: 15 Jan – 14 Mar 2027.
  - Likely Peak 3: mid-Apr to early Jul 2027. Akshaya Tritiya on 9 May is an "abujh muhurat" (no muhurat needed), so it is a big wedding day.
  - Peak 4: from 10 Nov 2027.
  - Exact Apr–Jul and Nov–Dec 2027 dates need a Drik city-specific lookup.
- **Agri calendar (general pattern, unverified this session):**
  - Rabi (wheat, gram, lentil, mustard; Sagar is a major gram/wheat district): sowing Oct–Dec, harvest and mandi sales Mar–Apr, with MP wheat procurement roughly from late Mar/Apr. Agri-input shops peak Oct–Nov (seed, DAP) and tractor/harvester promotions peak Feb–Apr.
  - Kharif (soybean, urad, paddy): sowing with the monsoon, mid-June–July; harvest Sep–Oct. Input shops peak in Jun–Jul.
  - Post-harvest cash in hand drives the Akshaya Tritiya and wedding spending in Apr–May, and the Navratri/Diwali spending in Oct.
- **Education calendar (general pattern, unverified):**
  - Board exams (CBSE / MP Board) run Feb–Mar. Results come Apr–May. Admissions and coaching enrolment peak Apr–Jul.
  - Competitive exam cycles (JEE/NEET) run Jan–May. Coaching institutes should promote in Mar–Jun and around Teachers' Day.
- **Festive retail peaks 2027:** Holi (mid-Mar), Navratri–Diwali (30 Sep – 31 Oct, earlier than in 2026), and Dhanteras 27 Oct for gold, utensils and vehicles. Pitru Paksha (16–29 Sep 2027) is traditionally a low period for new purchases.

- Apr–Dec 2027 vivah muhurat dates were not retrieved from an authoritative city-specific source (Drik for Sagar/Bhopal). Fetch them before planning wedding-season campaigns.
- MP Board 2027 exam timetable, CBSE 2027 date sheet and MP wheat procurement (MSP) start date for rabi 2027 were not found.
- Exact Holashtak 2027 dates (8 days before Holika Dahan, about 13/14–21 Mar 2027) were not sourced.
