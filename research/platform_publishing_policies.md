## 1. META – rules common to Instagram and Facebook
---------------------------------------------------------------------

### 1.1 Community Standards (removal-level) [OFFICIAL]
Source: https://transparency.meta.com/policies/community-standards/ (no update date on page; applies to Facebook, Instagram, Threads; explicitly "including AI-generated content")
27 categories. Most relevant to an agency:
- Spam (misleading links, engagement manipulation, repetitive posting, deceptive redirects)
- Prohibited Commercial Practices; Restricted Goods and Services (pharma, tobacco, alcohol, weapons, gambling, etc.)
- Hateful Conduct (religion/caste/ethnicity – key for festive & religious imagery)
- Privacy Violations (posting others' personal info / images without consent)
- Third-Party Intellectual Property Infringement; Using Meta IP and Licenses (don't misuse Meta/Instagram logos)
- Misinformation (incl. manipulated media); Authentic Identity Representation; Inauthentic Behavior
- Locally Illegal Content (India-specific legal takedowns apply)
Outcome: content removed, strikes against Page/account, possible feature limits / Page unpublishing.

### 1.2 Recommendation (reach) rules – Instagram [OFFICIAL]
Source: Instagram Creators – "Recommendations and originality" https://creators.instagram.com/recommendations-and-Originality (announcement dated 30 Apr 2024; page live 2026)
Content can stay up but be ineligible for Explore, Reels tab, Feed suggestions to non-followers:
- Sensitive content (self-harm, violence, sexually suggestive, regulated products like tobacco/vapes/adult products/medicinal drugs)
- Health & finance: cosmetic-procedure promotion, exaggerated health claims, "miracle cures", misleading financial models (payday loans etc.) – relevant if posting client work for clinics, gyms, finance advisors
- Political / social-issue content (not recommended by default to non-followers)
- Clickbait and engagement bait
- Low-quality/unoriginal content taken from other sources without adding material value
- Misinformation (fact-checked false)
- Eligibility conditions for recommendation: original, follows Community Guidelines, NO visible watermark (e.g. other apps' logos such as CapCut/TikTok marks), follows recommendation guidelines.
- Duplicates: when identical copies are detected, only the original is recommended; reposts get a label linking to the original creator.
- Aggregator rule: accounts posting others' content 10+ times in 30 days without substantial changes become ineligible for recommendations until they drop below 10 in 30 days. Check in Settings > Account Status (appeal possible).

April 30, 2026 update [OFFICIAL-via-report] – originality rules extended from Reels to photos and carousels. Adding a border, watermark, subtitles, crop/resize or a caption credit alone does NOT count as meaningful change; commentary, analysis, original graphics/voiceover does. Repeat offenders lose recommendations to non-followers (followers still see posts).
Sources: https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/ ; https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/414773/instagram-algorithm-update-discourages-reposted-co.html ; https://www.digitalmusicnews.com/2026/05/01/instagram-debuts-more-original-content-protections-for-creators/
(Exact official text not read – petapixel returned 403 – mark details as "reported".)

### 1.3 Recommendation / demotion – Facebook [OFFICIAL]
Source: "Types of content we demote" https://transparency.meta.com/features/approach-to-ranking/types-of-content-we-demote/ (page updated 2 Jul 2025)
Demoted: Clickbait links; Engagement bait; Fact-checked misinformation; Content likely violating Community Standards; borderline content (suggestive, profanity, gore) in personalised ranking. Repeat offenders demoted more.
Engagement bait = asking people to like/react/comment/share/tag/vote ("Comment YES", "Tag 3 friends", "Share to win", "React ❤️ for A, 😮 for B"). Genuine questions/requests for opinions are fine. (Category definitions date from Meta's Dec 2017 announcement; still listed in 2025 page.)
Recommendations inventory page "updated 24 Jun 2026": https://transparency.meta.com/features/explaining-ranking/fb-feed-recommendations/

Unoriginal content crackdown on Facebook (announced 14 Jul 2025) [OFFICIAL-via-report]: accounts that repeatedly reuse others' videos/photos/text without meaningful enhancement get reduced distribution and lose monetisation; Meta may link duplicates to the original. Transformative content (commentary, reaction, analysis) is OK.
Sources: https://techcrunch.com/2025/07/14/following-youtube-meta-announces-crackdown-on-unoriginal-facebook-content ; primary: https://creators.facebook.com/blog/combating-unoriginal-content (not read directly)

### 1.4 AI-generated content disclosure (Meta) [OFFICIAL-via-report + SECONDARY]
- Since 2024, Meta requires users to use its disclosure tool ("Add AI label" toggle on Instagram share screen / "AI info" label) when posting organic content with PHOTOREALISTIC VIDEO or REALISTIC-SOUNDING AUDIO that was digitally created or altered; penalties may apply for not disclosing. The requirement does not apply to still images, but Meta auto-labels images when it detects C2PA/IPTC AI metadata (e.g. from Adobe Firefly, OpenAI, Google tools, Meta AI).
- Label renamed "Made with AI" -> "AI info" (Jul 2024). Some surfaces may now say "AI content" [UNVERIFIED].
- Separate "AI-generated profile" label for accounts featuring AI people; unlabeled ones not recommended [SECONDARY].
Sources: https://about.fb.com/news/2024/02/labeling-ai-generated-images-on-facebook-instagram-and-threads/ (Clegg, Feb 2024; via press) ; https://www.axios.com/2024/02/06/meta-ai-labeling-fix-oversight-board
Practical: label any AI video/voiceover (AI avatar reels, AI voice in Hindi) – also required in India by IT Rules 2026 (section 6.6).

### 1.5 Music & copyright [OFFICIAL]
Meta Music Guidelines (effective 26 Mar 2024): https://www.facebook.com/legal/music_guidelines
- Music in commercial / non-personal content is restricted unless you hold the licence.
- No "music listening experience"; prefer short clips; content must have visual value beyond the audio.
- Violations: blocked, muted, removed; repeated -> Page/account deletion possible.
- Business/professional accounts: popular licensed library is restricted for many business accounts; use Meta Sound Collection (royalty-free, cleared for use on Meta) or properly licensed tracks. [SECONDARY – availability varies by country/category] https://www.facebook.com/sound/collection
- Branded/promotional Reels using trending Bollywood songs = high risk (copyright claim/mute and Indian Copyright Act exposure – see 6.8).

### 1.6 Branded content [OFFICIAL – Pages terms] + [SECONDARY]
- Facebook Pages Terms Policy 10: if the Page receives anything of value to promote another party, follow Branded Content Policies and disclose (use Paid Partnership tool). https://www.facebook.com/policies_center/pages_groups_events
- Branded content policy page (transparency.meta.com/.../branded-content-policies) returned 404 today – could not re-read. Known rules [GENERAL KNOWLEDGE]: tag the business partner with the branded content tool; prohibited items (illegal products, tobacco/vapes, weapons, adult products, certain supplements, MLM/get-rich offers, etc.); Meta's label does NOT replace ASCI's legal "Ad/#ad" requirement in India.
- Client-work showcases where FutureTech posts about its OWN services are not "branded content" (it's own promotion). If a client pays for/receives a feature on FutureTech's page, it is.

### 1.7 Contests/giveaways – Facebook Pages Terms [OFFICIAL]
Source: https://www.facebook.com/policies_center/pages_groups_events (Policy 7; no date shown)
- Organiser responsible for lawful running: official rules, offer terms, eligibility, any registration.
- Must include a complete release of Meta by each entrant and acknowledgement that the promotion is not sponsored/endorsed/administered by or associated with Meta (Instagram equivalent in Instagram Promotion Guidelines – same idea, Instagram).
- Must NOT require participants to share, repost, tag others or otherwise promote the promotion as a condition of entry ("share to enter", "tag 3 friends to enter" = not allowed; "comment to enter" / "like to enter" is generally allowed but "like" entry is engagement-bait-adjacent and demoted).
- India: lotteries/games of chance are regulated by state laws – keep giveaways skill/question-based or free-entry with clear T&C [GENERAL KNOWLEDGE]; ASCI Code requires material conditions of contests to be stated.
- Pages also cannot promote online gambling, addiction treatment, online pharmacies without Meta's written permission.

## 2. INSTAGRAM – specifics & technical specs
---------------------------------------------------------------------
Community Guidelines: https://help.instagram.com/477434105621119 (core: only share content you made or have rights to; no spam, fake engagement, buying likes/followers, nudity, hate, etc.)

Hashtags:
- Limit reduced from 30 to 5 per post/Reel caption; announced ~18 Dec 2025 by Instagram (@creators / Threads), Mosseri: "quality over quantity". Rolling out gradually; treat 5 as the rule. [OFFICIAL-via-report] https://betanews.com/2025/12/19/instagram-puts-a-limit-on-hashtag-usage/ ; https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-hashtags-five-per-post-10923075
- Whether hashtags placed in first comment count toward the limit: [UNVERIFIED].
- Hashtags don't boost reach per Mosseri; use 3–5 relevant (e.g. #DigitalMarketingTips #SmallBusinessIndia).

Links: captions don't make links clickable (link in bio, link stickers in Stories, up to 5 bio links) [GENERAL KNOWLEDGE]. Phone numbers allowed in captions/contact buttons (no Meta rule against), but spammy repetition = Spam policy.

Formats/specs:
- Feed images: 1080 px wide; ratios 1:1, 4:5 (1080x1350), 1.91:1; profile grid now shows 3:4 tiles (since Jan 2025) – 1080x1440 (3:4) upload now supported/recommended for portrait. [SECONDARY, multiple] https://www.businesstoday.in/technology/news/story/instagram-head-announces-big-changes-3-minute-reels-and-new-look-for-profile-grid-461527-2025-01-21
- Carousels: up to 20 photos/videos in-app (raised 2024); APIs/schedulers may still cap at 10; all slides take the first slide's ratio. [SECONDARY]
- Reels: 9:16, 1080x1920; max 3 minutes in-app (from Jan 2025). Keep key text inside the centre safe zone (avoid top ~14% and bottom ~20–35% UI overlay). [SECONDARY/OFFICIAL-via-report]. Report says Reels >3 min may not be recommended to new audiences [UNVERIFIED].
- Stories: 9:16, up to 60 s per segment [GENERAL KNOWLEDGE].
- Caption: 2,200 characters max [SECONDARY – widely cited, not confirmed on Help Center today]; first ~125 chars visible before "more".
- Alt text: supported – Advanced settings > Accessibility > Write alt text (new posts) or "Edit > Edit alt text" on existing posts; auto alt text exists. Length limit [UNVERIFIED] (100 chars old figure; API alt_text up to 1,000; not for Reels/Stories via API).
- Text on images: no Meta rule limiting text overlay for organic posts (the old 20% rule was ads-only and retired 2020) [GENERAL KNOWLEDGE].

## 4. GOOGLE BUSINESS PROFILE (GBP) POSTS
---------------------------------------------------------------------

### 4.1 Content policy for posts [OFFICIAL]
Source: "Business Profile photos, videos & posts content policy" https://support.google.com/business/answer/7213077 (no date on page; ©2026)
- Posts/photos subject to Maps "Prohibited & restricted content" policy.
- "To prevent fraud or abuse, Google may remove posts with unverified contact information, including phone numbers, email addresses, and social media handles." Use the "Call now" button (uses verified profile number) instead of typing numbers.
- Hotels cannot make Offer posts or mention deals/discounts (not relevant to agency).
Source: "Create posts" https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169 (©2026): "Phone numbers in a post description may get the post rejected if they can't be verified as belonging to the business."

Maps UGC Prohibited & Restricted Content: https://support.google.com/contributionpolicy/answer/7400114 (no date; ©2026)
- Spam/repetitive: same content posted multiple times (same or multiple accounts) – don't paste identical posts repeatedly; gibberish.
- Off-topic: general/political/social commentary, personal rants – posts must relate to the business.
- Advertising/solicitation (merchant posts): NO unrelated email addresses, phone numbers, social-media links or website links; offers and updates that seem promotional are allowed. => Promoting OTHER businesses / unrelated links = rejection risk. Posting about the agency's own services is allowed.
- Impersonation forbidden.
- Restricted: calls-to-action/offers to sell regulated goods (alcohol, gambling, tobacco, guns, health/medical devices, regulated pharma, adult, financial services) – no purchase links, contact details or deals for these.
- Also standard prohibited content: sexually explicit, offensive, dangerous, hate, harassment, IP infringement, personal information (no customer details/screenshots with PII).

Common rejection causes (practitioner reports) [SECONDARY]:
- Phone number in text (most consistently reported). Sterling Sky: number inside the image was not rejected "as of right now" – but Publer reports banner-image numbers can also fail. https://www.sterlingsky.ca/rejected-google-posts/
- Hashtags: not functional on GBP; some tools report they can cause failures – avoid. [SECONDARY]
- URL shorteners / multi-redirect links, links to unrelated sites. ALL CAPS, excessive emojis, repetitive CTAs, heavy text overlays / text-only graphics, stock or low-quality images, collages. No official text-overlay % (the "10%" figure is unofficial).
- Check the "?" next to "Not approved" to see which policy.

### 4.2 Post types, fields, CTA, lifetime [OFFICIAL]
Source: https://support.google.com/business/answer/7342169 ; API: https://developers.google.com/my-business/reference/rest/v4/accounts.locations.localPosts
- Update (STANDARD): description, photo/video, optional button + link.
- Offer: requires title, start/end dates & times; "View offer" button auto-added (custom CTA ignored); optional description, photo/video, coupon code, redeem-online link, terms & conditions.
- Event: title, start/end date & time required (no time = 24 h), optional description, media, button.
- (Alert type exists but not generally available.)
- CTA button options (API enum): BOOK ("Book"), ORDER ("Order online"), SHOP ("Buy"), LEARN_MORE ("Learn more"), SIGN_UP ("Sign up"), CALL ("Call now" – no URL; uses profile phone). GET_OFFER deprecated.
- Scheduling and recurring posts (weekly/monthly/custom) and multi-profile posting now supported in the post editor.
- Lifetime: "Posts older than 6 months are archived unless a date range is set." Offer/Event posts end at their end date. (The old "update posts expire after 7 days" rule is OUTDATED – ignore.)
- Statuses: Live / Pending / Not approved.
- Character limits [SECONDARY, consistent]: description 1,500 chars; Offer/Event title ~58 chars. Preview truncation varies (~80–300 chars) – put key message first.
- Q&A feature removed (API ended 3 Nov 2025; public Q&A phased out from Dec 2025, replaced by AI "Ask Maps" that draws on posts, descriptions, services). [SECONDARY, well-corroborated] => Posts now also feed Google's AI answers – keep them factual.

### 4.3 Photo/video specs [OFFICIAL]
Source: https://support.google.com/business/answer/6103862 (©2026)
- Photos: JPG or PNG, 10 KB–5 MB, recommended 720x720 px, minimum 250x250 px; in focus, well lit, no significant alterations or excessive filters, should represent reality.
- Videos: up to 30 seconds, up to 75 MB, 720p or higher.
- Media review can take 24–48 h. Violating media marked "Not approved".
- Post image practical recommendation [SECONDARY]: 1200x900 (4:3) – central crop safe; avoid text near edges.

### 4.4 Representation / eligibility [OFFICIAL]
Source: https://support.google.com/business/answer/3038177
- Business name = real-world name only (no taglines, keywords, phone, URL, service descriptions, location descriptors) – e.g. "FutureTech Digital Marketing Agency" OK, "FutureTech – Best SEO Company in Delhi" not OK.
- Service-area businesses hide address; no virtual offices; co-working only if staffed & signed during hours.
- Agencies managing client profiles: must have owner consent (manager access); no explicit ban on an agency posting about its own services – an agency with a staffed office is eligible like any business.
- Generic tips posts: allowed if business-relevant (e.g. "3 Google Business tips from FutureTech") – not "off-topic"; avoid political/social commentary and pure memes. No explicit rule found about generic tips [inferred from policy].

## 6. INDIA – LEGAL / SELF-REGULATORY
---------------------------------------------------------------------

### 6.1 ASCI Code [OFFICIAL]
Source: https://www.ascionline.in/the-asci-code (Chapter I signed 10 Jul 2025; Ch. III updated 29 Apr 2024; Ch. IV 2012). ASCI Code Book PDF: https://www.ascionline.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/07/Code-Book.pdf
Applies to advertising including the agency's own promos on social media and to agencies.
- Ch. I Truthful & honest: objective claims must be substantiable (keep proof of "100+ clients", "#1 agency", "3x leads" etc.); no misleading by implication/omission; "free" only if no hidden cost; investment/contest material conditions stated; health claims restrained; references to a person/firm/institution need permission (client logos/testimonials need client consent).
- Social media posts by media/advertisers must carry upfront disclosure "Advertisement/Ad/Sponsored/Partnership/Collaboration" where it's paid content.
- Ch. II decency; Ch. III no deriding religion, caste, gender, body shape etc.; no exploitation of children.
- Ch. IV Fair competition: comparative claims allowed only if factual, clear, substantiable, not misleading, no denigration; no copying other ads' layout/slogans/music; no unjustifiable use of competitor name/goodwill.
- Testimonials (ASCI practice/guidelines) [GENERAL KNOWLEDGE]: must be genuine, current, from real users, represent typical results; fabricated/AI-fake testimonials = misleading. Before/after: must be genuine, not digitally enhanced, typical, state time period/conditions; ASCI and DoCA treat filtered beauty before/afters as misleading.

### 6.2 ASCI Influencer guidelines [SECONDARY + OFFICIAL press release]
- Launched May 2021 (effective 14 Jun 2021): any material connection (payment, free product, discount, employment, relationship) must be disclosed with an upfront, prominent label: #ad, Ad, Advertisement, Sponsored, Collaboration, Partnership, Employee, Free gift. Platform "Paid partnership" tool alone not enough; bio disclosure not enough; label must not be buried in hashtags or under "more"; for video, on-screen label for adequate duration + audio for audio-only content.
- Virtual (AI) influencers must disclose they are not human [SECONDARY, 2023].
- Aug 2023 health/finance update: https://www.ascionline.in/wp-content/uploads/2023/08/Health-and-Finance-Guidelines-Update-Press-Release.pdf ; April 2025 Addendum 2 relaxed: generic health/finance content OK without qualifications; technical advice (e.g. stock/fund recommendations) needs SEBI registration/credentials displayed. [SECONDARY]
- Consumer Affairs (DoCA) "Endorsements Know-hows" guidelines for influencers, Jan 2023, + Aug 2023 health addendum; CCPA June 2025 advisory on influencer self-audit [SECONDARY].
Implication for FutureTech: if the agency pays creators or clients get free services in exchange for testimonial reels, label "Ad/Collaboration" upfront in caption AND on video.

### 6.3 Consumer Protection Act 2019 & CCPA rules [OFFICIAL-via-report]
- CCPA Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Misleading Advertisements and Endorsements, 2022 (bait ads, "free" claims, surrogate ads, disclaimers must not hide material info, endorser due diligence) [GENERAL KNOWLEDGE – not re-read].
- Guidelines for Prevention and Regulation of Dark Patterns, 2023 (30 Nov 2023): 13 dark patterns incl. FALSE URGENCY ("only 2 slots left", fake countdown), basket sneaking, confirm shaming, forced action, subscription trap, interface interference, bait & switch, drip pricing, DISGUISED ADVERTISEMENT, nagging, trick question, SaaS billing, rogue malware. Applies to platforms, advertisers and sellers. 5 Jun 2025 advisory: e-commerce platforms to self-audit within 3 months. https://www.barandbench.com/law-firms/view-point/dark-patterns-in-india-from-hidden-practices-to-regulatory-spotlight ; https://taxguru.in/corporate-law/ccpa-mandates-e-commerce-self-audits-dark-patterns.html
Implication: Diwali/festive offers must be real (real deadline, real original price for "50% off", no fake scarcity, total price incl. GST disclosed, T&C visible).

### 6.4 National Flag / Emblem / names [OFFICIAL-legal texts]
- Emblems and Names (Prevention of Improper Use) Act, 1950: no use of National Flag, name/emblem of Govt of India, state govts, UN, WHO, Mahatma Gandhi / PM name, Ashoka Chakra etc. (Schedule items) for trade, business, trademark or design without permission. https://www.indiacode.nic.in (Act 12 of 1950)
- State Emblem of India (Prohibition of Improper Use) Act, 2005 – S.4 bars use of the State Emblem (Lion Capital + "Satyameva Jayate") for trade/business/profession, trademarks or designs; penalties under S.7 (imprisonment up to 2 yrs and/or fine up to Rs 5,000; up to 2 yrs + higher fine for repeat – figures [GENERAL KNOWLEDGE], verify). https://www.indiacode.nic.in/handle/123456789/2038
- Flag Code of India 2002 (amended 30 Dec 2021 polyester/machine-made; 20 Jul 2022 day & night display) + Prevention of Insults to National Honour Act 1971: Flag must not be used for commercial purposes, as costume/drapery, printed on napkins/cushions/disposable items, with lettering on it, or displayed upside-down/torn [GENERAL KNOWLEDGE – MHA Flag Code text]. https://www.mha.gov.in/sites/default/files/flagcodeofindia_070214.pdf
Implication: Independence/Republic Day creatives – use tricolour-inspired colour palettes and abstract motifs, NOT the actual flag with agency logo/offer text on it, NOT the Ashoka emblem, NOT PM/Gandhi images implying endorsement.

### 6.5 Religious sensitivity [GENERAL KNOWLEDGE]
- Bharatiya Nyaya Sanhita 2023: s.196 (promoting enmity between groups), s.299 (deliberate and malicious acts intended to outrage religious feelings) – replaced IPC 153A/295A from 1 Jul 2024.
- ASCI Ch. III: no deriding religion. Meta Hateful Conduct + Google offensive content.
- Practice: no deities on discount banners/footwear-adjacent placements, no humour about gods/rituals, no mixing deities with sales CTAs; balanced festival coverage (Diwali, Eid, Christmas, Gurpurab, Onam, Pongal etc.) with respectful tone.

### 6.6 AI content labelling – IT (Intermediary Guidelines) Amendment Rules 2026 [OFFICIAL-via-report]
- Notified 10 Feb 2026, effective 20 Feb 2026. Defines "synthetically generated information" (audio/visual/audio-visual artificially created or altered to appear authentic; text excluded).
- Significant social media intermediaries (Meta, YouTube etc.) must make users declare whether content is synthetic before publishing, verify via technical tools, and label it prominently with provenance metadata. => On Instagram/Facebook in India, use the AI disclosure toggle for any realistic AI image/video/voice; false declarations risk removal.
Sources: https://www.hoganlovells.com/en/publications/india-introduces-mandatory-labelling-for-ai-and-3hour-takedown-for-illegal-content ; https://blog.ipleaders.in/it-rules-2026/
[UNVERIFIED: exact final wording on label size/duration; whether still images are covered identically.]

### 6.7 DPDP Act 2023 + DPDP Rules 2025 [OFFICIAL]
- Rules notified 13/14 Nov 2025 (PIB: https://static.pib.gov.in/WriteReadData/specificdocs/documents/2025/nov/doc20251117695301.pdf). Phased: Board provisions immediate; consent-manager provisions from ~Nov 2026; core notice/consent/security/rights obligations from ~13 May 2027 (18 months). Penalties up to Rs 250 crore per breach category (Act Schedule).
- Personal data includes faces in customer photos, names, phone numbers, WhatsApp chats, reviews with names, lead lists in screenshots.
- Act exempts personal data made publicly available by the data principal themselves (S.3(c)(ii)) – but don't rely on it for marketing reuse.
- Practice now (good practice; legally binding from May 2027): written consent/release from clients for case-study screenshots & logos; written consent from customers/employees whose faces/names appear; blur names, numbers, emails, order IDs in dashboard/WhatsApp/review screenshots; children's photos need verifiable parental consent; honour removal requests; keep consent records.
- Also IT Act 2000 s.66E (privacy of images) and contractual NDAs with clients [GENERAL KNOWLEDGE].

### 6.8 Copyright (India) [GENERAL KNOWLEDGE]
- Copyright Act 1957: images, music (lyrics/composition – IPRS; sound recordings – labels/PPL), videos, fonts (font software licensed per EULA), illustrations are protected; fair dealing (s.52) is narrow (criticism/review/news) and does not cover promotional use.
- Bollywood/regional film songs in business reels = infringement risk + Meta muting/removal; use Meta Sound Collection, licensed stock music (with commercial social licence), or original audio.
- Stock photos/icons/fonts: keep licences (Canva Pro/Free licence terms, Freepik attribution, Google Fonts OFL OK). Don't use Google Images/Pinterest finds or celebrity photos (also personality rights – Delhi/Bombay HC orders 2023–2025 protecting celebrities' names/likeness incl. AI voice clones).
- Trademarks: don't use other brands' logos (e.g. Google, Meta logos) implying partnership; "Google Partner"/"Meta Business Partner" badges only if actually certified, per their badge guidelines.
