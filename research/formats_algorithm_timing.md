# Social Media Formats, Algorithm Signals, Frequency and Timing (Instagram / Facebook / Pinterest, 2025–2026) — for FutureTech Digital Marketing Agency, Sagar MP

Context: the agency's audience is local MP small-business owners. Its own audit found Hindi video reached 5–10x more than English static images, and posting was inconsistent (2–5 posts in some months, none in others). Research date: Nov 2026. Most of the sources are 2025–2026 and are labelled; older material is flagged.

## 1. What ranking signals do Instagram and Facebook prioritise in 2025–2026?

### Takeaway
Instagram's head, Adam Mosseri, names **watch time, likes per reach and sends (shares) per reach** as the top three ranking signals (Jan 2025). Likes matter more for reaching followers, and sends matter more for reaching non-followers. **Originality** is now enforced on both Instagram and Facebook: reposted or aggregated content loses recommendations. Facebook Feed ranks posts by predicting whether a user will comment, share or start a discussion.

### Cited Findings
- Mosseri (video series, reported 22 Jan 2025): "The top three signals that matter most for ranking are watch time, likes and sends." He told creators to watch "average watch time, likes per reach, and sends per reach" in Insights. — [Social Media Today, 22 Jan 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/)
- Mosseri: "Likes are slightly more important for connected content, and sends are slightly more important for unconnected content." Connected content reaches followers; unconnected content is recommended to non-followers. — [Social Media Today, 22 Jan 2025](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/)
- Comments were not among Mosseri's top three. Social Media Today's commentary (not Mosseri) suggests carousels may rank well because they hold attention longer. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/)
- Indian trade coverage of the same Instagram guidance on what boosts reach — [Social Samosa](https://www.socialsamosa.com/news-2/instagram-reveals-key-factors-for-boosting-reach-8646916)
- Originality, Instagram (older, 2022): Mosseri said reposted content, and accounts that repost, are less likely to be recommended, and that Instagram would stop over-valuing aggregators. — [Verge, Apr 2022 via link](https://link.finimize.com/external/27476953.29476/aHR0cHM6Ly93d3cudGhldmVyZ2UuY29tLzIwMjIvNC8yMC8yMzAzNDM1Ni9pbnN0YWdyYW0tcmFua2luZy1yZWVscy1vcmlnaW5hbC1jb250ZW50LXRpa3Rvaz91dG1fY2FtcGFpZ249ZGFpbHktZ2xvYmFsLTI1LTA0LTIwMjImdXRtX3NvdXJjZT1lbWFpbCZ1dG1fbWVkaXVtPURhaWx5JTIwR2xvYmFsJTIwRWRpdGlvbiUyMFVzZXJz/5e8216d1d3b0f1056656c408Bd2fe6c95); [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/?p=166721)
- Originality, Instagram (Apr 2026, trade reports): the repost and aggregator penalty now reportedly covers photos and carousels as well as Reels. The penalty is reported as no recommendations for unoriginal aggregators. Mosseri suggested that meaningful transformation keeps eligibility: voiceover, green screen, your own text or commentary, or a Collab with the original creator. — [PetaPixel, 30 Apr 2026](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/); [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/?p=192084). *I could not open the PetaPixel page (HTTP 403), so these details come from search snippets.*
- Originality, Facebook: Meta announced a crackdown on "unoriginal" content on 14 Jul 2025, defined as repeatedly reusing another creator's work without credit. Repeat offenders lose monetisation, and their distribution is reduced. About 10M impersonator profiles were removed. — [MediaNama, Jul 2025](https://www.medianama.com/2025/07/223-meta-cracks-down-on-unoriginal-facebook-content/); [Engadget](https://www.engadget.com/social-media/meta-says-its-cracking-down-on-facebook-creators-who-steal-content-203713569.html?sfnsn=mo); [exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/digital-news/meta-updates-policies-to-reward-original-creators-and-limit-reposted-content-152931.html)
- Facebook, Mar 2026 update: Meta tightened its definition of "original". Content that only makes minor edits to someone else's work, or is duplicative, is deprioritised. So are reaction-only videos, stitched clips and narration of what is already on screen. Meta reportedly said the deprioritisation **doubled views and watch time for original Reels** in H2 2025 compared with the previous year. — [TechCrunch, 13 Mar 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/13/facebook-makes-it-easier-for-creators-to-report-impersonators/); [MediaPost](https://www.mediapost.com/publications/article/413530/); [ProPakistani, 15 Mar 2026](https://propakistani.pk/2026/03/15/facebook-moves-to-reward-original-creators-crack-down-on-copycats/)
- Facebook Feed ranking (Meta Transparency Center): a four-step process of inventory, signals/predictions, relevance score and ordering. It uses thousands of signals and predicts how likely you are to comment, how likely your friends are to comment if you share, and how likely a post is to start back-and-forth discussion. The page was updated June 2025. — [Meta Transparency Center](https://transparency.meta.com/features/ranking-and-content); [Meta Newsroom, Jun 2023 (older)](https://about.fb.com/news/2023/06/how-ai-ranks-content-on-facebook-and-instagram/)
- Meta publishes AI "system cards" for Feed, Reels and Stories (14 for Facebook). It says the signals change frequently and it does not disclose ones that could be gamed. — [Meta AI blog](https://ai.meta.com/blog/how-ai-powers-experiences-facebook-instagram-system-cards/)

### Inferences
- For FutureTech the most useful metric to track per post is **sends/shares per reach**, because non-followers are the people it wants to reach (new SMB owners). Content that a shop owner would forward to a friend or a WhatsApp group (a Hindi tip, a GST/Google Business Profile how-to, a local case study) suits this signal.
- Watch time favours the Hindi video that already outperforms in the audit. English static images get no watch-time signal and few sends.
- The agency should never repost trending clips or other creators' videos unchanged. Everything should be filmed or designed in-house, with no watermarks from other apps.

### Gaps
- I found no primary Mosseri post or creators.instagram.com page from 2026 that restates the signal ranking. The Jan 2025 video is the latest primary-quoted statement found.
- Official Instagram weightings for saves and comments are not published.
- I found no official Meta statement on Reels-specific signal weights for Facebook.

## 2. Format benchmarks: Reels vs carousels vs static images

### Takeaway
By engagement rate per follower (Socialinsider, 2025 data), **carousels (0.55%) ≈ Reels (0.52%) > images (0.37%, down 17% YoY)**. For small accounts (1–5K followers), carousels averaged the most views per post (993), ahead of Reels (580) and images (417). Static single images are the weakest format and are still declining. On Facebook, Reels are the strongest format by engagement.

### Cited Findings
- Socialinsider 2026 Instagram benchmarks (published 20 Feb 2026, 2025 data, about 67.5M posts from about 579K brand accounts). Engagement rate by followers:
  - Reels: 0.50% in 2024, 0.52% in 2025
  - Carousels: 0.55% in both years
  - Images: 0.45% in 2024, 0.37% in 2025 (−17%)
  - Overall: 0.50% in 2024, 0.48% in 2025

  — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram)
- The same page's quarterly update gives Q3 2026 rates of Reels 0.48%, carousels 0.50%, images 0.35% and overall 0.45%. *Caveat: the page has internal inconsistencies. It states "−24%" overall while its own table shows about −4%, and its date range is odd.* — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram)
- Small accounts (1–5K followers), 2025:
  - Average views per post: Reels 580, carousels 993, images 417
  - Average comments: Reels 3, carousels 2, images 1
  - Follower growth for the year: 22% (38% in 2024)

  — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram)
- Brands shifted their mix toward Reels. Monthly posts in 2024 vs 2025 were Reels 6→8, carousels 4→5 and images 10→7, so Reels went from about 30% to about 40% of posts (my calculation). — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram)
- Reels get the most comments at every follower tier. — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram)
- Conflicting third-party figures (Colorlib cites Reels 1.23% and carousels 0.99% "per Socialinsider") contradict Socialinsider's own page. Treat them as unreliable. — [Colorlib](https://colorlib.com/wp/instagram-engagement-rate/)
- Facebook (Socialinsider 2026):
  - Average Page engagement is about 0.15% of fans (25M posts, 130K pages).
  - Reels are the strongest Facebook format, at 0.13% in Q2 2026 (down from 0.15%).

  — [Socialinsider Facebook benchmarks](https://socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/facebook)

### Inferences
- The best format split for FutureTech is **Hindi Reels for reach and discovery, plus Hindi or Hinglish carousels** (step-by-step tips, checklists, before/after audits) for saves and for views at small-account size. Single English static images should be retired or kept to a minimum (festival greetings only).
- Engagement-rate benchmarks are per follower. At small follower counts, 1–3 comments per post is normal, so the agency should judge success by reach, sends and enquiries (DMs and WhatsApp clicks) rather than likes.

### Gaps
- I found no reliable 2025–2026 *reach-rate by format* benchmark for Instagram. Socialinsider reports views, not reach.
- I found no India-specific format benchmark.
- I did not fetch Hootsuite's or Later's 2025–2026 format data directly.

## 3. Optimal posting frequency and consistency effects

### Takeaway
Buffer's study (Aug 2025; 2.1M posts, about 102K accounts) found **3–5 feed posts per week is the sweet spot**. Weeks with **zero posts carry a measurable "no-post penalty"**, and even 1–2 posts a week clearly beats nothing. Posting more did not hurt reach per post.

### Cited Findings
- Buffer, "How often should you post on Instagram" (Kirsti Lang, data scientist Julian Winternheimer, 13 Aug 2025). The sample was about 2.1M feed posts (photos, Reels and carousels pooled; Stories excluded) from about 102K accounts. — [Buffer](https://buffer.com/library/how-often-to-post-on-instagram/)
  - Extra follower-growth rate compared with not posting (fixed-effects model):
    - 1–2 posts/week: +0.12%
    - 3–5 posts/week: +0.26%
    - 6–9 posts/week: +0.44%
    - 10+ posts/week: +0.66%

    For a 5K-follower account this is about 6, 13, 22 and 33 extra followers per week.
  - Median reach per post compared with 1–2 posts/week: about +12% at 3–5, +18% at 6–9 and +24% at 10+. The biggest jump is from 1–2 to 3–5 posts.
  - Weeks with no posts showed growth about 0.08 standard deviations below each account's own norm (the "no-post penalty").
  - Higher frequency did not appear to hurt reach or cause unfollows. Buffer stresses that quality comes first and that the schedule should be sustainable.
  - The findings are correlational, and formats were not separated.
- Secondary coverage of the same study — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/study-shows-posting-more-instagram-leads-to-more-reach/757633/); [WebsiteBuilderExpert](https://www.websitebuilderexpert.com/news/buffer-instagram-posting-study/)
- Socialinsider's 2025 brand averages were about 20 Instagram feed posts per month (about 8 Reels, 5 carousels and 7 images). — [Socialinsider](https://www.socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/instagram)

### Inferences
- FutureTech's history (2–5 posts a month, with some zero months) is well below even Buffer's 1–2 posts/week "maintenance" level, and every zero week triggers the measured penalty. A realistic target is **4 feed posts a week** (for example 2 Reels and 2 carousels) plus Stories on 3–5 days. The schedule should be batch-produced monthly and queued in Buffer so that no week is empty.
- Consistency matters more than volume: a sustained 3–4 posts a week beats bursts of 10 followed by silence.

### Gaps
- I found no frequency study specific to Facebook Pages or India for 2025–2026.
- I found no data on how often to post Stories.

## 4. Best posting days and times for Indian audiences (IST)

### Takeaway
No rigorous India-only dataset exists. Global data (Sprout 2026) favours **Tue–Wed, roughly 12–6 PM local time**. India-focused guides point to **evenings around 6–9 PM IST** for Reels, plus weekday afternoons. Treat both as starting hypotheses and confirm them in the account's own Insights.

### Cited Findings
- Sprout Social (updated Mar 2026; about 2B engagements across about 307K profiles):
  - Instagram: Mon 2–4 PM, Tue 1–7 PM, Wed 12–9 PM and Thu 12–2 PM (local time). Avoid weekends and early mornings.
  - Facebook: Tue–Wed 12–8 PM.
  - Across platforms: Tue–Wed 12–6 PM.

  — [Sprout Instagram](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/best-times-to-post-on-instagram/); [Sprout cross-platform](https://sproutsocial.com/insights/best-times-to-post-on-social-media/)
- Recurpost (about 2M Instagram engagements, June 2025): the top slot was **Thursday 9 PM**, with Sunday and Wednesday 8–10 PM close behind. This is a vendor dataset and not confirmed to be India-only. — [Recurpost](https://recurpost.com/?p=3544)
- WSCube Tech (Indian edtech blog, 2026 edition):
  - Weekdays around 2–5 PM IST, with Tue–Thu best and Saturday worst.
  - Reels at 6–9 PM IST, after work or classes.
  - Its methodology is weak.

  — [WSCube](https://www.wscubetech.com/blog/instagram-post-time/)
- Timetopost suggests 5:30, 6:30 and 7:30 PM IST, but says its curve is **global**, not segmented to India. — [Timetopost](https://timetopost.co/best-time-to-post/instagram/india/)
- How to find the account's own best times: in Instagram Insights, go to Total followers and then Most active times, which shows followers' activity by hour and day. Buffer's analytics or best-time features can supplement this. *This is standard product knowledge; I did not open a help page for it in this session.*

### Inferences
- The audience is shop owners, agri-input dealers, clinic doctors and coaching operators, whose phone time differs from office workers'. Likely windows to test:
  - **Early afternoon lull, 1:30–3:30 PM IST**, when shops are quiet after lunch.
  - **Night, 8:30–10:30 PM IST**, after shops close (many MP retail shops close around 9 PM).
  - Agri-input dealers are busy in the morning during sowing seasons (kharif June–July, rabi Oct–Nov).
- A simple test: rotate Reels between 2 PM and 9 PM slots for 4 weeks, then compare reach in the first 24 hours. Use "Most active times" for followers, but remember that recommended Reels reach non-followers, whose activity Insights does not show.

### Gaps
- I found no published India-specific (IST) dataset with a transparent methodology.
- I found nothing on tier-2/3 or SMB-owner daily phone-usage patterns. The windows above are my inference.

## 5. Reel best practices: length, hook, captions, audio, aspect ratio, originality, Trial Reels

### Takeaway
Make **original, vertical 9:16 Reels with a strong first-3-second hook and burned-in on-screen captions**, and keep them tight. Use audio that fits the message (trending audio may give a short boost; original voiceover holds up better for saves and shares). Never repost watermarked or unedited third-party clips. Use **Trial Reels** to test hooks on non-followers.

### Cited Findings
- Watch time is a top-three ranking signal (Mosseri, Jan 2025), so retention (hook plus pacing) is the core lever. — [Social Media Today](https://www.socialmediatoday.com/news/instagram-shares-algorithm-insights-2025/738034/)
- Hook in the first 3 seconds; on-screen text and captions because many people watch muted, which also helps accessibility. These are practitioner guides, not official Instagram guidance. — [Hootsuite blog](https://blog.hootsuite.com/instagram-reels/); [Sendible 2025](https://sendible.com/insights/instagram-reels); [Opus](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-instagram-reels-length)
- Trending audio may give a short discovery bump in roughly the first 48 hours, while original audio tends to do better for saves and shares. Audio should fit the content. This comes from third-party blogs. — [Quso](https://quso.ai/blog/instagram-reels-best-practices.html); [Sendible](https://sendible.com/insights/instagram-reels)
- Length: practitioner guidance is to test 30, 60, 90 and 120 s versions and keep most Reels under 3 minutes. Blogs give conflicting "maximum length" claims (90 s vs 3 min). — [Opus](https://www.opus.pro/blog/ideal-instagram-reels-length); [Castmagic](https://www.castmagic.io/blog/how-long-can-an-instagram-reel-be)
- Originality and reposting penalties: see Q1. Reposted content is not recommended, and transformation (voiceover, own commentary, Collab) is the accepted route. — [Tubefilter](https://www.tubefilter.com/?p=192084); [PetaPixel, Apr 2026](https://petapixel.com/2026/04/30/new-instagram-policies-target-reposted-content/)
- Trial Reels (launched late 2024) are shown first to non-followers only. Mosseri said they usually perform worse than normal Reels because they always reach a new audience, so compare Trial Reels only with other Trial Reels. — [Fanpage Karma](https://www.fanpagekarma.com/insights/instagram-reels-algorithm/)
- A report says Trial Reels expanded in July 2025 to public professional accounts with 1,000+ followers. I could not confirm this from a primary source. — [Fanpage Karma](https://www.fanpagekarma.com/insights/instagram-reels-algorithm/)
- Buffer supports Trial Reels in its Reel composer, with Auto or Manual "share to followers" options. Trial Reels cannot be combined with collaborators. — [Buffer Help](https://support.buffer.com/article/657-scheduling-instagram-posts-and-reels)

### Inferences
- Suggested Reel template for FutureTech:
  - Length: 20–45 s.
  - 0–3 s: a Hindi spoken hook plus a bold text overlay naming the pain point, for example "Dukaan Google pe kyun nahi dikhti?" ("Why doesn't your shop show up on Google?").
  - Body: 3 steps.
  - Close: a CTA to WhatsApp.
  - Hindi on-screen subtitles throughout; 9:16, 1080×1920.
  - Original voiceover by the founder or team. Trending audio only at low volume under the voice.
- Use Trial Reels for A/B tests of hooks once the account qualifies.

### Gaps
- I found no official creators.instagram.com page in this session confirming the current maximum Reel length, the optimal length, or the aspect-ratio and safe-zone specs. These came from general knowledge and third parties: 9:16, 1080×1920, and keeping text out of the bottom ~20% where the UI overlays sit.
- No data on whether Hindi subtitles specifically lift retention.

## 6. Facebook Page reach in India, what still works, and whether Pinterest is relevant

### Takeaway
Organic reach on Facebook Pages is low: about 1.25% of followers on average, but about **4.35% for small Pages with 1–5K followers**. Reels are the best-performing Facebook format, and original Reels are now favoured after Meta's 2025–26 originality push. For a tier-2/3 Indian SMB audience, Facebook's value lies in Reels cross-posted from Instagram, local groups and WhatsApp sharing. Pinterest is growing in APAC/India but is a low priority for a local B2B/SMB agency.

### Cited Findings
- Facebook organic reach (Socialinsider data cited by Webtonic): about 1.25% of followers on average. By Page size, 1–5K followers reach 4.35% and 100K–1M reach 0.50% (872K-post study). — [Webtonic, 2026](https://www.webtonic.io/blog/social-media-reach-statistics); [Socialinsider Facebook benchmarks](https://socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/facebook)
- Facebook Page engagement is about 0.15% of fans. Reels are the top format at 0.13% in Q2 2026. — [Socialinsider](https://socialinsider.io/social-media-benchmarks/facebook)
- Meta reports that deprioritising unoriginal content doubled views and watch time for original Reels on Facebook in H2 2025 compared with the year before. — [TechCrunch, Mar 2026](https://techcrunch.com/2026/03/13/facebook-makes-it-easier-for-creators-to-report-impersonators/); [ProPakistani](https://propakistani.pk/2026/03/15/facebook-moves-to-reward-original-creators-crack-down-on-copycats/)
- Facebook Feed predicts shares and whether "your friends are likely to comment if you share", so shareable posts are rewarded. — [Meta Transparency Center](https://transparency.meta.com/features/ranking-and-content)
- WhatsApp has an estimated 532M users in India (2025), described as mass-market across tier-2/3 with strong Hindi and vernacular usage. This comes from a marketing blog with no primary source. — [GrowthJockey](https://www.growthjockey.com/blogs/top-social-media-platforms)
- Pinterest reached 619M global MAU in Q4 2025 (+12% YoY), according to the company's earnings release. — [BusinessWire, 12 Feb 2026](https://www.businesswire.com/news/home/20260212059914/en/Pinterest-Announces-Fourth-Quarter-and-Full-Year-2025-Results-Delivers-14-Revenue-Growth-and-Record-Users)
- APAC added about 20M Pinterest users in 2025, "primarily from India, Indonesia and the Philippines". This is a secondary aggregator. — [market.us](https://market.us/statistics/social-media/pinterest/)

### Inferences
- **Facebook:** cross-post every Instagram Reel to the Facebook Page (Buffer or Meta's native cross-posting). Share useful posts into local Sagar and MP business or trader Facebook groups where the group rules allow it. Use Hindi captions. The 4.35% small-Page reach figure suggests a small Page is not useless.
- **WhatsApp:** each Reel or carousel should also go out as a WhatsApp Status and to the agency's broadcast list or Channel. WhatsApp shares also feed the "sends" signal when shared from Instagram.
- **Pinterest:** low priority. Its audience is inspiration and design-led (home, fashion, recipes) rather than local SMB owners looking for marketing services. At most, auto-repurpose carousels and infographics as Pins for long-tail search traffic to blog posts, through Buffer. Do not put production effort into it.

### Gaps
- I found no India-specific Facebook Page reach benchmark for 2025–2026.
- I found no official India Pinterest MAU figure or Pinterest B2B data for India.
- I found no WhatsApp Business or Meta India data on SMB reach via WhatsApp sharing.

## 7. Content mix and pillars for small agencies; tier-2/3 and vernacular insights

### Takeaway
Indian internet growth is rural and Indic-language-led: 98% of users consume Indic-language content, and 57% of *urban* users prefer regional languages. An agency case study aimed at tier-2/3 SMB owners found a **Hindi version reached about 5x the English version, 94% of it organic**, which matches FutureTech's own 5–10x audit finding. Pillars should be Hindi or Hinglish, education-led, and include local proof.

### Cited Findings
- IAMAI–Kantar "Internet in India 2024" (published Jan 2025):
  - 886M active users (+8% YoY), of which 488M are rural (55%).
  - 98% accessed content in Indic languages.
  - 57% of urban users prefer regional-language content.
  - 13% of non-users cite the lack of local-language content as a barrier.

  — [Business Today, 16 Jan 2025](https://www.businesstoday.in/amp/technology/news/story/indias-internet-revolution-key-insights-from-kantar-and-iamai-report-461043-2025-01-16); [Storyboard18](https://www.storyboard18.com/digital/surge-in-indic-language-adoption-leads-to-55-surge-in-rural-internet-users-53632.htm); [Entrepreneur India](https://india.entrepreneur.com/news-and-trends/internet-users-in-india-set-to-cross-900-mn-led-by-indic/485634)
- The 2025 IAMAI–Kantar edition reportedly gives 958M active users, 57% (about 548M) of them rural. This has a single source and is unverified. — [LexisNexis page](https://beta.lexisnexis.co.uk/?p=2924)
- Social Beat case study for Cholamandalam Finance, whose audience is SMB owners in tier-2/3 cities: an English animated video reached 409,609 people, and the Hindi version reached about 2.2M views, 94% of them organic. The figures are self-reported. — [Social Beat](https://socialbeat.in/blog/cholamandalam-finance-social-beat-tap-vernacular-content)
- Short video, vernacular content and regional creators are shaping India's digital economy. — [exchange4media](https://www.exchange4media.com/marketing-news/short-videos-vernacular-content-regional-creators-shaping-indias-digital-economy-139104.html)
- A creator-economy report found 68.2% of Indian creators produce mainly in Hindi. — [Apparel Resources](https://apparelresources.com/business-news/retail/indias-influencer-marketing-industry-nears-us-500-million-says-report/)

### Inferences
Suggested pillar mix for about 16 feed posts a month (my synthesis, not benchmarked):
1. **Educational Hindi tips (~35%):** Google Business Profile, WhatsApp Business catalogue, festive offers, Meta ads basics, reviews. Use Reels and carousels; these are the most "sendable" posts.
2. **Local case studies, before/after and results (~20%):** client screenshots of calls or leads, a GBP before/after, a website redesign. Use carousels plus Reels.
3. **Founder-led or face-to-camera video (~15%):** opinions and myth-busting in Hindi. This builds trust in a relationship-driven tier-2 market.
4. **Client testimonials (~10%):** video in the client's own Hindi words at their shop or clinic.
5. **Behind the scenes and team (~10%):** shoots at local businesses in Sagar.
6. **Offers, festive and CTA posts (~10%):** Diwali, Navratri and season-specific packages, each with a WhatsApp CTA.

Segment-specific angles for agri-input kendras, clinics and coaching institutes should be tied to their seasons (kharif and rabi sowing, admission season, health camps).

### Gaps
- I found no rigorous benchmark of content pillars for agency accounts specifically.
- I did not verify the IAMAI–Nielsen "68% prefer native language" (2024) claim; I found only a secondary citation.
- I found no MP-specific social usage data.

## 8. Buffer scheduling notes for Instagram, Facebook and Pinterest

### Takeaway
Buffer can auto-publish Instagram feed posts, Reels, carousels (including mixed image and video) and Trial Reels to **Instagram Professional (Business or Creator) accounts**. A first comment works **only with automatic publishing**, not notification publishing. Some features still fall back to notification or mobile publishing.

### Cited Findings
- Scheduling a first comment is only available when publishing automatically to Instagram Professional accounts. It is entered in the "first comment" field. — [Buffer Help: Using Instagram with Buffer](https://support.buffer.com/article/554-using-instagram-with-buffer); [Buffer Help: Scheduling posts, reels, stories](https://support.buffer.com/article/657-scheduling-instagram-posts-and-reels)
- Carousels are supported, including mixed image and video. A carousel draft with more than 20 tags may fail to publish. — [Buffer Help](https://support.buffer.com/article/657-scheduling-instagram-posts-and-reels)
- Buffer's help gives conflicting hashtag guidance: one article mentions up to 5 hashtags in the caption and up to 30 in the first comment. Check the current composer. — [Buffer Help](https://support.buffer.com/article/554-using-instagram-with-buffer)
- Trial Reels can be scheduled from the Reel composer (Auto or Manual share to followers), but not combined with collaborators. — [Buffer Help](https://support.buffer.com/article/657-scheduling-instagram-posts-and-reels)

### Inferences
